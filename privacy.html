不收集任何数据。本 App 不含账号系统、不含统计分析、不含广告，不会把你的查询内容、文本或个人信息上传到我们的服务器（我们也没有服务器）。

在设备本地处理的内容

词典查询、文本分析、手写识别、例句、导入的电子书和学习进度全部在你的 iPhone 上完成并保存在本机。你在「我的词库」中添加的词条只保存在本机，只有当你主动导出并分享文件时才会离开设备。

可选的第三方服务

以下功能默认关闭，只有在你自己填入服务商的 API Key 并使用时才会联网：
・AI 服务（设置 › AI 服务）：使用「AI 讲解」「AI 解释」或阅读器的 AI 翻译时，你选中的日语文本会发送给你自己选择的服务商（例如 Anthropic、OpenAI、Google、DeepSeek、阿里云、Moonshot、智谱、火山引擎、SiliconFlow、OpenRouter、Groq、Mistral、xAI、Microsoft Azure，或你填写的自定义地址）。
・云端朗读（发音设置）：需要朗读的日语文本会发送给你选择的语音服务商（Microsoft Azure、OpenAI、Google Cloud 或 ElevenLabs）。
这些请求由你的设备直接发往服务商，不经过我们；数据的处理方式受该服务商的隐私政策约束。API Key 只保存在你设备的钥匙串或本机设置中，不会发送给我们。不填写 Key 时，App 完全离线运行。

系统功能

「翻译」菜单集成、iOS 离线翻译和系统词典均由 Apple 在设备上提供，受 Apple 的隐私政策约束。

ENGLISH
We do not collect any data. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your searches, texts and personal information are never uploaded to us; we run no servers.

On-device processing

Dictionary lookups, text analysis, handwriting recognition, examples, imported e-books and study progress are processed and stored only on your iPhone. Words you add to My Words stay on the device unless you export and share the file yourself.

Optional third-party services

These features are off by default and only go online if you enter your own API key and use them:
• AI service (Settings › AI Service): when you use AI explain or AI translation, the Japanese text you selected is sent to the provider you chose (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud, Moonshot, Zhipu, Volcengine, SiliconFlow, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, xAI, Microsoft Azure, or a custom endpoint you entered).
• Cloud voices (Pronunciation): the Japanese text to be read aloud is sent to the speech provider you chose (Microsoft Azure, OpenAI, Google Cloud or ElevenLabs).
Requests go directly from your device to the provider and never pass through us; the provider's own privacy policy applies. API keys are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Without a key the app runs fully offline.

System features

The Translate menu integration, on-device translation and the system dictionary are provided by Apple on the device and covered by Apple's privacy policy.
